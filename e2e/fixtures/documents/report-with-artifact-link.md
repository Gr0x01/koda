# Spring seed starting

Zone 8a, last frost lands somewhere in the first half of April most years. Everything below counts
backward from that date, so a warm March moves the whole schedule and a cold one does not.

## Under the lights

| Crop | Weeks before last frost | Tray | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tomato | 6 | 72-cell | Pot up once at true leaves |
| Pepper | 8 | 72-cell | Bottom heat until germination |
| Basil | 4 | 50-cell | Hates a cold windowsill |
| Squash | 2 | 4-inch | Direct sow if March stays warm |

Peppers are the long pole. If they miss their window they set fruit in the heat, and the whole
summer's harvest slides a month.

## Hardening off

1. Two hours of shade, then back inside.
2. Add an hour a day, morning sun first.
3. Skip a day whenever the wind picks up; wind sets them back further than cold does.

For the dates against a specific frost day, open the
[frost date explorer](interactive-report.html) and drag the frost slider to whatever the forecast is
saying this week.
